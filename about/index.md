## Who I Am

I'm Felipe Gonzalez Roldan, a technology professional bridging the worlds of software development, artificial intelligence, and education. With a background in Mechatronics Engineering and a Master's in Industrial Automation, I've spent over 8 years building AI systems, developing software solutions, and teaching the next generation of programmers. Currently, I work as a Senior Python Developer at Globant while maintaining my commitment to education as a university lecturer.
